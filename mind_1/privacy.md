# Privacy Policy for Mind Ember Games

_Last updated: 2026-11-11. Developer: Active Mind Craft. Contact: ggprouser@gmail.com_

Mind Ember Games (package `com.activemindcraft.mindember`) is a single-player puzzle and block game hub for adults. It has no account system and no server of its own.

## Data stored on your device
Game progress, best scores, coins, streaks, challenges, achievements, settings and purchase status are stored locally on your device. We cannot see this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows banner ads (only before and after a game, never during play) and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect approximate location (from your IP address), ad interaction data, diagnostics and device identifiers to serve, measure and secure ads. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy. Where required (for example the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent before ads are requested; you can review your choice in Settings.

## Purchases
Optional purchases (coin packs, Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

## Notifications
If you opt in, the app schedules a local daily-reward reminder on your device. No push service is used. You can turn it off in Settings or in Android settings.

## Analytics, accounts and servers
No analytics, crash-reporting or backend service. The app does not use Firebase and does not ask for your name or email. Only Google AdMob and Google Play communicate over the network.

## Google Play Games
Sign-in with Google Play Games is optional and off by default. If you sign in, your player ID and your best score in each game (and, if the app offers it, the achievements you earn) are sent to Google Play Games for that game's leaderboard, and your progress may be saved in your Google Play Games saved data. Zen runs never send a score. If you do not sign in, scores stay on your device. The app works fully without it.

## Children
The app is for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

## Your choices
Reset your advertising ID in Android settings; use the privacy options in Settings; uninstall the app to remove local data.
