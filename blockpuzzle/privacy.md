# Privacy Policy for Block Puzzle

_Last updated: 2026-11-05. Developer: Active Mind Craft. Contact: ggprouser@gmail.com_

Block Puzzle (package `com.activemindcraft.blockpuzzle`) is a single-player puzzle game. It has no account system and no server of its own.

## Data stored on your device
Game progress, best score, coins, streaks, challenges, achievements, settings and purchase status are stored locally on your device. We cannot see this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
Block Puzzle shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ad requests are marked as child-directed and limited to the highest content rating (G). The app does not request or use your advertising ID (the permission is removed from the app). AdMob may still collect approximate location (from your IP address), ad interaction data and diagnostics to serve, measure and secure ads. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Where required (for example the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can review your choice any time from "Ad privacy options" in Settings when it is shown.

## Purchases
Optional purchases ("Remove Ads", coin packs) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

## Notifications
If you opt in, the app schedules a local daily-reward reminder on your device. No push service is used and no data is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings or in Android settings.

## Sharing
If you tap a Share button, Android opens its share sheet with a short text and a picture of your score (game name, score, best score and the current look of the game). You choose the app that receives it. Block Puzzle itself sends nothing, and the picture contains no name or ID.

## Analytics, accounts and servers
We do not run any analytics, crash-reporting or backend service, and we do not ask for your name, email or other personal details. The app does not use Firebase. Only Google AdMob and Google Play (billing) communicate over the network.

## Google Play Games
This version does not use Google Play Games sign-in. If a later version adds it, this policy will be updated first and the feature will be optional.

## Children
Block Puzzle is a family-friendly game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads are requested with child-directed treatment.

## Permissions
Internet (ads and purchases), notifications (optional reminder), run at startup (restore the reminder after reboot).

## Changes and contact
We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: ggprouser@gmail.com.
