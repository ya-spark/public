# Privacy Policy for Craft Ranger Games

_Last updated: 2026-11-11. Developer: Active Mind Craft. Contact: ggprouser@gmail.com. Package: com.activemindcraft.craftranger._

Craft Ranger Games is a learning game for young people in Years 7 to 10. It has no account system and no server of its own that stores anything about players.

**What the app stores on your device:** player profiles (a nickname you type, a built-in picture, a year level), learning progress, stars and coins, settings and purchase status. This stays on the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. It is not sent to us. We recommend a nickname that is not a real name.

**Learning content:** the app fetches learning content (question packs) from one fixed web address, using plain downloads. The app sends nothing about the player or the device to that address; like any web request it can be seen by the host as an ordinary connection from your network.

**Advertising:** the app is directed to children, so its ads are set to child-directed treatment with the lowest content rating (G). It shows one small banner, only before and after a game, never during play. There are no pop-up ads and no ads that give rewards. Ads are served by a child-safe ad network through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may use device and app information to serve and measure ads; for child-directed ads it does not show personalised ads. Parents can remove ads with a one-time purchase. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

**Purchases:** purchases (coin packs, Remove Ads, the Full Unlock and extra content packs) are for a parent and sit behind a "grown-up" question. They are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive payment details.

**Notifications:** an optional reminder is a local notification on the device. No data is sent for it.

**Sharing and links:** the app has no chat, no social features, no user-made content and no sign-in. Anything that leaves the app opens only after the grown-up question.

**Analytics and servers:** no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no Firebase. We do not collect, sell or share personal information from children.

**Children:** this app is directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think we have, contact us and we will help you remove it.

**Your choices:** use the parent area to remove a player profile, remove ads, and set Wi-Fi-only downloads; uninstall the app to remove all local data.

**Changes:** we may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
