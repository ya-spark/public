# Privacy Policy for Craft Champion Games

_Last updated: 2026-11-11. Developer: Active Mind Craft. Contact: ggprouser@gmail.com. Package: com.activemindcraft.craftchampion._

Craft Champion Games is a revision game for students in Years 11 and 12. It has no account system of its own and no server of its own that stores anything about students.

**What the app stores on your device:** profiles (a nickname you type, a built-in picture, a year level), learning progress, stars and coins, settings and purchase status. This stays on the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. It is not sent to us. We recommend a nickname that is not a real name.

**Learning content:** the app fetches learning content (question packs) from one fixed web address, using plain downloads. The app sends nothing about the student or the device to that address; like any web request it can be seen by the host as an ordinary connection from your network.

**Advertising:** the app's ads are set to child-directed treatment with a low content rating (PG at most). It shows one small banner, only before and after a game, never during play. There are no pop-up ads and no ads that give rewards. Ads are served by a child-safe ad network through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may use device and app information to serve and measure ads; with this treatment it does not show personalised ads. A one-time purchase removes ads. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

**Google Play Games (optional):** you can sign in with Google Play Games to save progress in the cloud and use a leaderboard. If you do, Google processes your sign-in under its own policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the app uses only your Play Games player name and progress for those features. The app works fully without signing in.

**Purchases:** purchases (coin packs, Remove Ads, the Full Unlock and extra content packs) sit behind a "grown-up" question. They are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive payment details.

**Notifications:** an optional reminder is a local notification on the device. No data is sent for it.

**Sharing and links:** the app has no chat, no social features and no user-made content. Anything that leaves the app opens only after the grown-up question.

**Analytics and servers:** no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no Firebase. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

**Young users:** we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think we have, contact us and we will help you remove it.

**Your choices:** use the parent area to remove a profile, remove ads, and set Wi-Fi-only downloads; uninstall the app to remove all local data.

**Changes:** we may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
