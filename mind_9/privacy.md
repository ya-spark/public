# Privacy Policy for Mind Presto Games

_Last updated: 2026-11-11. Developer: Active Mind Craft. Contact: ggprouser@gmail.com. Package: com.activemindcraft.mindpresto_

Mind Presto Games is a collection of casual games. It has no account system and no server of its own.

**What the app stores on your device:** your scores, coins, unlocked games, daily reward and streak progress, challenge and achievement progress, settings and purchase status (for example whether ads were removed). This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. It is not sent to us.

**Advertising:** The app shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner on the home screen and between games, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information and ad interaction data to serve, measure and protect against fraud in ads. The app declares the `AD_ID` permission for this. In the EEA, UK and other regions where required, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings where shown. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

**Purchases:** Purchases (remove ads, coin packs) are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment details.

**Notifications:** If you turn on the daily reward reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No data is sent anywhere for this.

**Sharing:** If you tap Share, Android opens its share sheet with a short text and a picture of your score. You choose the receiving app. The app itself sends nothing.

**Google Play Games:** Sign-in is optional and off by default. If you sign in, your player ID and your best score in each game (and any achievements the app mirrors) are sent to Google Play Games for that game's leaderboard and handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. Without sign-in, scores stay on your device.

**Analytics and servers:** No analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no Firebase, no backend. We do not collect, sell or share personal data ourselves.

**Children:** The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

**Your choices:** Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android settings; turn off reminders; uninstall the app to remove all local data.

**Changes:** We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
